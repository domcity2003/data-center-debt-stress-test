Assumptions and limits

  • Starting point is KBRA's July 2026 net cash flow of $60.1m. The 2023 presale's 2026 and 2027 expirations ($30.8m at CH3, $55.8m across both buildings) scale the rent gains and losses. The 2026 anchor and the presale rent roll cannot be reconciled suite by suite, so the NCF bridge is a sensitivity proxy.
  • 85% of rent changes flow to NCF. Other cash flow is held at the 2026 level apart from the explicit stresses.
  • Takeout is the smallest of three limits: 67% LTV, 1.35x DSCR and 10% debt yield. DSCR uses a 30-year monthly amortizing constant. Proceeds are net of 1% fees.
  • Existing coverage is NCF over the current interest-only coupon. It is not the refinance DSCR.
  • No default probabilities, certificate losses or sponsor liquidity. Signed CH3 lease terms through August 2033 are the first item to confirm in diligence.
  • Illustrative underwriting research, not investment advice. Sources: KBRA, July 2026, KBRA, July 2025, S&P presale, 2023.
